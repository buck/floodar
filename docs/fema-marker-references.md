### Notes

- The report's central facts are confirmed in independent sources: the $56,000 federal/state
  project (Chronicle 2011 and ProPublica 2016), FEMA funding (ABC13 2011), and Blackburn's
  account of the Clear Lake sign (Baker Institute 2018).
- The report itself flags what is **not** documented: the program's grant number, the marker's
  exact locations and dimensions, its source model, and its vertical datum. The floodar
  write-up treats those as unknown.
