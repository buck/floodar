Research the history of the FEMA/Texas storm-surge markers installed in the Clear Lake/Houston area around 2010–2011 following Hurricane Ike.

I am building an augmented-reality project that can recreate these markers and visualize historic and future flooding. I need a source-backed historical and technical dossier, not just a general summary.

Please investigate:

1. ORIGIN AND PROGRAM

* What agency or agencies initiated the marker program?
* What was the official program name?
* What FEMA grant/program funded it?
* What roles were played by FEMA, the Texas Division of Emergency Management/TDEM, City of Houston, Harris County, or other entities?
* Total program cost and cost per marker if available.
* How many markers were planned statewide, regionally, and specifically in the Clear Lake area?
* When were they designed, installed, and removed?

2. CLEAR LAKE INSTALLATIONS
   Identify every known Clear Lake/Houston-area marker location.

For each location, provide:

* street/location description from contemporary sources
* exact or best-estimate latitude/longitude
* confidence level for the coordinates
* evidence supporting the location
* date installed and date removed, if known
* photographs or video showing the marker in place

Pay particular attention to:

* Clear Lake City Boulevard near Space Center Boulevard
* Bay Area Boulevard near Clear Lake High School
* Clear Lake Park or other nearby locations if contemporary sources mention them

Distinguish confirmed installations from planned-but-never-installed locations.

3. PHYSICAL DESIGN
   Find as much information as possible about the marker's construction and appearance:

* total height
* width
* shape/profile
* colors
* graduated height markings
* wording and graphics
* Category 4 / Category 5 labels
* indicated storm-surge elevations
* material
* manufacturer or fabricator
* designer
* engineering drawings, specifications, bid documents, procurement records, or presentation slides

I am particularly interested in reconstructing the marker accurately as a 3-D AR object.

Search for photographs from multiple angles and identify the highest-resolution surviving images.

4. SURGE DATA AND SCIENTIFIC BASIS
   Determine what flood/surge model or planning scenario generated the heights shown on the markers.

Investigate:

* whether the levels came from SLOSH, HAZUS, a hurricane evacuation study, FEMA flood studies, TDEM modeling, or another source
* exact modeled water-surface elevations, surge depths, or datums if available
* what "Category 4" and "Category 5" meant on the marker
* whether the approximately 20–25 foot levels represented storm surge, elevation above sea level, or depth above local ground
* whether the cited scenario involved a hurricane entering Galveston Bay/Houston Ship Channel
* what storm track, size, forward speed, tide, or other assumptions were involved

Clearly separate facts documented at the time from later interpretations.

* who made the final decision to remove the markers

Quote important statements sparingly and provide direct source links.
